The brief

Platform teams register a Terraform or OpenTofu module once and anyone can stand up their own stack from it. The mark should feel sober and precise, belong next to the Geist-set UI, and stay clear of the category's purple, cubes, clouds and planets.

Final design

Planned stack

Two applied layers and one outlined: the layer the plan will add.

  • Stacks are the product's core noun; the outline is the plan you review before apply
  • One colour: the outline, not a colour, marks the planned layer, so it survives any ink
  • Wordmark is the app header's Geist Semibold, outlined, so logo and product read as one
  • Pixel-fitted 16 and 32 px cuts keep the hollow layer visible in a browser tab
